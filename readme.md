# Goals

Define cycling goals.

- Distance
- Duration
- Calories
- Power
- Average power
- Normalized power
- Speed
- Average speed
- Cadence
- Average cadence
- Heartrate zone
- Average heartrate zone
- Intensity Factor
- Traning Stress Score
- Total ascent
- Total descent

Display up to 10 goals as:

- Vertical bars
- Horizontal bars
- Proportional segments

With optional label and value.
Different options per datafield size possible.

## Presets

Use presets to easy set the individual goals.

- First select distance or duration
- Define suffer factor
- Choose:
    - Recovery
    - Casual
    - Weight loss
    - Endurance
    - Cardio
    - Short and fast
- Click again to apply

## Advanced

Cadence counter: x seconds not pedaling will show details.

HSP breakpoint: 1-255. Default 127.5, decision breakpoint to display white or black text on a color. Colors below HSP breakpoint will have white text, the others black text.
If some colors have black text but still appear dark (garmin color + sunglasses), consider changing HSP to a higher value (ex. 150) so more colors are considered dark.

Show HSP value: Show calculated hsp value in top right corner (only for Proportional segments).

Focus field count: Display actual value for max number of fields.
Focus start at: Only focus fields if ratio is greater than this value.

Color schemes:

Scheme 1: The "Infrared" (Thermal/Intensity)
If this datafield tracks something like power, heart rate, or intensive anaerobic work, you want colors that match physiological strain. This scheme starts cool, hits a "cruise control" sweet spot, transitions to an alert phase, and finishes in a high-danger deep purple/magenta zone.

0% – 50% (Warmup): Light Charcoal/Grey to Soft Teal Blue.

50% – 100% (The Work Zone): Teal Blue to Vibrant Green. Hit 100% right as it locks into solid green.

100% – 150% (The Overdrive): Vibrant Green to Neon Orange.

150% – 200%+ (The Redline): Neon Orange to Deep Magenta/Violet. (Magenta stands out better than dark red on a bike computer against black or white background grids).

[0%] Grey ───> Teal ───> [100%] Green ───> Orange ───> [200%+] Magenta

Scheme 2: The "Cyberpunk/Aero" (High Contrast)
Designed specifically to take advantage of the Edge 1050's punchy color reproduction. It uses bright neon colors that look highly modern and offer massive contrast changes at major milestones so a cyclist can read it instantly with a 0.5-second glance.

0% – 50%: Deep Navy Blue to Electric Cyan.

50% – 100%: Electric Cyan to Bright Lime Green.

100% – 150%: Bright Lime Green to Neon Hot Pink. (Hitting pink instantly alerts the rider they have broken past the 100% barrier).

150% – 200%+: Neon Hot Pink to Pure White or Laser Lemon Yellow.

[0%] Navy ───> Cyan ───> [100%] Lime ───> Pink ───> [200%+] Pure White

Scheme 3: The "Medal Podium" (Gamified Achievement)
If your progress metric is a daily goal, distance, or climbing target, treating it like a game keeps it highly motivating. This uses standard luxury reward colors.

0% – 75% (Building): Matte Copper/Bronze gradient.

75% – 100% (Closing In): Bronze to Sleek Chrome/Silver.

100% – 150% (Goal Achieved): Silver to Bright Gold.

150% – 200%+: (Elite Tier): Bright Gold to a shimmering Electric "Diamond" Blue or Pearl Mint.

[0%] Bronze ───> [75%] Silver ───> [100%] Gold ───> [200%+] Diamond Blue

Scheme 4: The Pastel Logic
0% – 50%: Soft Muted Grey to Calm Powder Blue.

50% – 100%: Calm Powder Blue to a Pale Mint/Sage Green.

100% – 150%: Pale Mint to a Soft Creamy Peach/Chalky Coral.

150% – 200%+: Soft Peach to a Muted Lavender.

[0%] Soft Grey ───> Powder Blue ───> [100%] Pale Mint ───> Creamy Peach ───> [200%+] Lavender
